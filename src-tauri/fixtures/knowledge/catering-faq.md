# Catering FAQ

## Delivery

Free delivery inside [CITY] for orders of 30 boxes or more.
Orders under 30 boxes pay a delivery fee based on distance.

## Packages

Paket Hemat and Paket Komplit are priced per box, with a minimum order of 20 boxes.
Paket Komplit includes rice, vegetables and chicken or fish.

## Payment

We accept bank transfer and cash on delivery. A deposit of half the total confirms the order.
